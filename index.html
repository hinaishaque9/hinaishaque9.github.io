🔬Research

M.S. thesis, Bahria University

Improved vs traditional stoves

Quantified health risks from PM10 and PM2.5 released by biomass burning in slum areas. Used Verified Carbon Standard methodology and cost-benefit models to weigh the economic and environmental gains of switching to improved cookstoves.

Extended research, Bahria University

Microplastics in kitchen air

Used Raman and FTIR spectroscopy to identify microplastics in PM10 and PM2.5 from rocket and traditional stoves. Rocket stoves produced significantly lower microplastic and particulate concentrations, which means lower exposure and better indoor air.

Voluntary toxicology research

Water quality and fish, Indus River

Measured pH, TDS and heavy metals (ICP-MS, AAS) to assess toxic effects and bioaccumulation in fish near the Dasu hydropower project.

MSc thesis, University of the Punjab

Purification of Swollenin

Investigated how Swollenin improves cellulase efficiency for biofuel production, using microbial biotechnology methods.
